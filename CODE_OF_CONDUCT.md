# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want depkeeper to be a harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We act in ways that keep the community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

---

## Our standards

Good behaviour:

- Show empathy and kindness.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give feedback kindly, and accept it gracefully.
- Own mistakes, apologise, and learn.
- Think about what is best for the whole community.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Sharing someone's private information (such as an address) without permission
- Anything else a reasonable person would see as inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Responsibilities of maintainers

Maintainers explain and enforce these standards, and act fairly when someone breaks them.

They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do
not follow this Code of Conduct. They will explain why when it is appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and
releases. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public, for example
through an official account or at an event.

---

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository's
Security tab:

**<https://github.com/rahulkaushal04/depkeeper/security/advisories/new>**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Community leaders are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

---

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers use these steps to decide the consequences of a violation.

| Level | Behaviour | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | Inappropriate language or conduct that is unprofessional or unwelcome | A private written warning that explains the problem. A public apology may be asked for. |
| 2. Warning | A single serious incident, or a pattern of misconduct | A warning with consequences. No contact with the people involved, including in project spaces, for a set period. Breaking this may lead to a ban. |
| 3. Temporary ban | Serious violation, or sustained inappropriate behaviour | A temporary ban from all project interaction for a set period. Breaking the terms may lead to a permanent ban. |
| 4. Permanent ban | A pattern of violations, harassment of a person, or aggression toward groups | A permanent ban from all project spaces. |
